Sketch & Throw

privacy policy · effective 27 September 2026

Sketch & Throw traces a pot from a photo, measures it, shows it in 3D and estimates the clay needed to throw it. It is made by Ferdinand Kirchner ("I"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

Photos and camera

Photos you pick and pictures you take with the live camera are processed on your phone. They are never uploaded by the app. When you save a vessel, its photo, traced outline and settings are stored in the app's private storage on your phone, where other apps cannot read them. The app asks for camera permission only when you open the live camera, and uses it only to show the preview, detect objects in it and take a picture when you press the shutter.

Diagnostics sent by Google ML Kit

The live camera and the first guess at where the vessel sits in a photo use Google's ML Kit object detection, which runs on your phone. ML Kit sends Google information about how the detector runs, not your photos. According to Google, this is:

  • device information: manufacturer, model, operating system version and build, and available machine-learning hardware
  • app information: package name and app version
  • a per-installation identifier, which does not identify you or your device
  • performance metrics, API configuration such as image format and resolution, input and output sizes, event types and error codes

Google uses this to maintain and improve ML Kit, sends it encrypted over HTTPS and does not transfer it to third parties. ML Kit also uses Firebase remote configuration and Firebase installations for its diagnostics. See Google's ML Kit data disclosure and the Google Privacy Policy.

What the app does not do

  • no accounts or sign-in
  • no advertising and no advertising identifiers
  • no analytics or tracking of its own
  • no sale or sharing of your data

Deleting your data

Delete a vessel in the app with the delete key in the vessel library, or from the save dialog of an open vessel. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on your phone. Diagnostics already sent to Google are handled under Google's policies linked above.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
